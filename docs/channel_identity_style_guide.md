# *Mahadev & Me*: channel identity and style guide

> **Visual style update (pilot v2):** the series now uses the painted art from the approved character sheet (anime/webtoon look, defocused backgrounds, glow and particles). The flat vector rig mentioned in older notes was retired.


## Premise
A modern young Indian woman talks to Mahadev about problems young people face today. Through
simple conversation, questions, gentle humour and compassion, he helps her see one thing
differently. **One episode, one lesson.** The relationship is an evolving friendship and
mentorship, not a series of sermons.

## Voice and tone
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Mahadev **asks** before he answers ("Aur agar sab theek ho gaya toh?") | Lecture or moralise |
| Let her **push back**, doubt or misunderstand once per episode | Make her a passive listener |
| Warm, a little playful, unhurried | Thunderous, intimidating, theatrical echo |
| Simple Hindi/Hinglish, lines under about 60 characters | Sanskrit-heavy or long sentences |
| Agency: what she will *do* next | "Just have faith" as a substitute for real help |
| Help-seeking note on heavy topics (Tele-MANAS 14416) | Dismiss trauma, abuse or mental-health needs |

## Honesty rules
* All dialogue is **original fiction** (`attribution: original_fiction` on every line).
* A real teaching may be quoted only with `attribution: scripture` **and** a `source:` citation.
  QA fails any scripture-like line ("Gita mein likha hai...") that has no source.
* Never write "Shiva said..." about invented lines.

## Episode structure (flexible)
0–4 s hook → 4–12 s conflict → 12–28 s Mahadev's guidance (question, analogy or humour) → her
pushback → 28–36 s realisation → 36–40 s moral card. Total **30–40 s**, enforced automatically.

## Visual style
* Painted anime/webtoon illustration taken directly from the approved character sheet, upscaled and cut out, with subtle 2.5D life motion.
* Palettes: **night** (indigo #1d2340, warm fairy-light amber #ffcf73); **sunset** (#33265a →
  #d0607a → #ffcf7a); **dream** (lavender #8a6bb8, blush #f5b9c8); Mahadev blue #8091c4 with gold
  #e0ab3c.
* Mahadev keeps his own light: the scene grade affects him at 35% strength, with a soft aura.
* Movement in every shot: blinks, lip-sync, breathing, hair sway, head motion, gesture changes,
  slow camera push or drift, and parallax backgrounds. QA fails any shot without motion.
* Transitions: cut, dissolve, and a *glow* flash for Mahadev's appearance.

## Typography and captions
* Captions: **Poppins Bold 56 px**, white with a dark outline on a soft dark pill. The active word
  is tinted by speaker: girl pink #ffb7cf, Mahadev gold #ffd36b.
* Optional English line: Poppins Regular 34 px.
* Titles and moral: **Kalam Bold**. Series logotype: **Yatra One**. All fonts are SIL OFL.
* Safe zone: captions sit at y ≈ 1370–1560 of 1920. They stay clear of the top 13% and bottom 17%,
  where Instagram and YouTube UI sits.

## Sound
* Original procedural score: tanpura-like drone, soft pad and sparse bells. Sunset episodes add a
  bansuri-like motif. It swells at the realisation.
* Music sits about 16 dB under dialogue and ducks automatically. The final mix is −14 LUFS with
  true peak ≤ −1 dBTP.
* SFX are light: phone scroll, sigh, divine shimmer on appearance, bloom chime, soft whoosh.

## Recurring metaphors (Mahadev's iconography)
Crescent moon: imperfection that still shines · Serpent: calm strength and boundaries · Ganga:
flow and letting go · Damru: rhythm and patience · Kailash: stillness · Flowers blooming in
their own season.

## Series packaging
Title card "MAHADEV & ME • EP n" plus the episode question for the first 3 s. A moral card with
the logotype at the end. The social caption ends with "(Original fictional story – Mahadev & Me)".
