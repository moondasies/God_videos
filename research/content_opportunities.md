# Content opportunities for *Mahadev & Me*

> **Basis:** these come from the series brief and general short-form storytelling practice. They
> are **not** derived from measured data on the two inspiration channels, which could not be
> accessed (see `channel_1_analysis.md` and `channel_2_analysis.md`). Treat each one as a
> hypothesis to test with your own analytics.

## Positioning gaps we can own (hypotheses)

1. **A recurring human protagonist** whose life continues between episodes (job hunt, a friendship
   repair, family talks), so viewers return to see what happens to *her*, not only for the lesson.
2. **Mahadev as a friend who asks questions** rather than delivering verdicts. Each reel turns on
   one reframing question ("Aur agar sab theek ho gaya toh?").
3. **Modern problems first, mythology second**: promotions, "seen" messages, rejection mails, LinkedIn
   comparison, parents and marriage pressure, burnout.
4. **Honest spirituality**: dialogue is clearly original fiction; real teachings are only quoted
   with citations; heavy topics carry a help-seeking line (Tele-MANAS 14416).
5. **Recurring visual metaphors tied to Mahadev's iconography**:
   * the crescent moon: imperfection that still shines;
   * the serpent: calm strength and boundaries;
   * the Ganga in his hair: flow, letting go;
   * the damru: rhythm and patience;
   * Kailash: stillness.
6. **A signature format beat**: the girl pushes back once in every episode. This keeps scripts out
   of sermon territory.
